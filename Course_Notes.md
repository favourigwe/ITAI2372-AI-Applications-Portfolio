# ITAI 2372 Course Notes

## Recent Advancements in AI

- Generative AI can create text, images, audio, video, and code.
- Multimodal AI systems can process several types of information.
- AI continues to expand across healthcare, finance, transportation, government, retail, and manufacturing.
- Responsible development requires attention to privacy, bias, transparency, accountability, and security.

## Healthcare and Agriculture

- AI supports medical imaging, diagnosis, patient-risk prediction, and personalized treatment.
- Agriculture uses AI for crop monitoring, pest detection, irrigation, yield prediction, and precision farming.
- Reliable and representative data is necessary to prevent biased or inaccurate outcomes.

## Finance and Fraud Detection

- Financial institutions use machine learning to monitor transactions and identify suspicious behavior.
- AI can assign transaction risk scores in real time.
- Important concerns include privacy, explainability, fairness, cybersecurity, and false positives.

## Smart Cities and Transportation

- AI supports traffic optimization, public transportation planning, environmental monitoring, and predictive maintenance.
- Connected vehicles communicate with infrastructure and other vehicles.
- Smart-city systems must balance efficiency with citizen privacy.

## Entertainment and Media

- AI assists with music, writing, art, design, video production, and recommendation systems.
- Deepfakes create opportunities in entertainment but also increase risks involving misinformation and consent.
- Ownership, authorship, transparency, and job displacement remain important concerns.

## AI and Blockchain

- Blockchain provides secure, transparent, and tamper-resistant records.
- AI can use blockchain data for fraud detection, forecasting, and decision support.
- Important concepts include smart contracts, Proof of Work, Proof of Stake, and decentralized systems.

## Retail

- AI supports recommendations, chatbots, visual search, virtual try-ons, automated checkout, and inventory forecasting.
- Computer vision helps with shelf monitoring and loss prevention.
- Retail AI raises concerns involving customer tracking, personalized pricing, bias, and workforce changes.

## AI Ethics

- Ethical AI should protect privacy, reduce bias, support transparency, and maintain accountability.
- Human oversight remains essential in high-stakes industries.
- AI systems should be monitored throughout their full lifecycle rather than only during development.

## Government and Public Sector

- Governments use AI for transportation, healthcare planning, public safety, citizen services, and resource allocation.
- Singapore's Smart Nation Initiative demonstrates a connected approach to digital government.
- Public trust depends on transparency, cybersecurity, accessibility, and responsible governance.

## Manufacturing

- Smart factories use machine learning, computer vision, robotics, Industrial IoT, predictive analytics, and digital twins.
- Predictive maintenance reduces downtime.
- Computer vision improves quality control.
- AI can improve sustainability by reducing material waste and energy use.

## Artificial General Intelligence

- Current AI is generally designed for narrow or specialized tasks.
- AGI refers to a system capable of learning, reasoning, adapting, and applying knowledge across many different tasks.
- Potential benefits include better coordination and decision-making.
- Major concerns include control, accountability, cybersecurity, bias, privacy, and overreliance.
